# Map display metadata

## Source and visibility

The bundled `world.geojson` and `us-states.geojson` are Natural Earth political-boundary data. Rendered map scenes include a visible Natural Earth source credit and a brief political-boundary disclaimer. If a host supplies an external GeoJSON collection, the renderer labels its source as supplied GeoJSON rather than claiming Natural Earth provenance; the host remains responsible for showing the collection's actual source and attribution where applicable.

## Political-boundary display condition

Political boundaries are shown for neutral geographic visualization only. Their inclusion, names, lines, and omissions are not a statement, recognition, endorsement, or position on sovereignty, jurisdiction, or border delimitation. Rendering reflects the source data and scale and must not be treated as political or legal authority. Public-domain copyright status only addresses permission to use and redistribute the data; it does not establish political-display approval, regional compliance, or suitability for any particular jurisdiction. China-specific maps are not implemented.
